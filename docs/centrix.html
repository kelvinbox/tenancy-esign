Centrix credit check filler

Fills the Centrix Consumer Credit Report form with an applicant from Tenant Management in one click.

Set up (once per computer)
  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B.
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    Fill Centrix
Each credit check
  1. In Tenant Management, open the application, then Credit check > Copy for Centrix.
  2. On the Centrix credit report page, click Fill Centrix on the bookmarks bar. The first time, allow it to see the clipboard (or paste when it asks).
  3. The filled boxes turn yellow. Check them and the report options, then press GO yourself.

The details go straight from your clipboard into the Centrix page. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Can't drag it? , add a new bookmark called "Fill Centrix", and paste the code as its URL.
